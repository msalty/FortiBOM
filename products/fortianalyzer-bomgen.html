Architecture Patterns
🖥 Single Tier Standalone
Single FortiAnalyzer unit. All features supported. Cost-effective and simple. No redundancy — scheduled backups recommended.
Best for: SOHO and SMB with limited devices where redundancy is not a requirement.
🔄 Single Tier High Availability (HA)
Up to 4 cluster members. Real-time log and data synchronization. Distributes reporting/FortiView load. Purchase 2× the same FAZ model. Each unit must also have its own FortiAI subscription (if used).
Best for: SMB/SME seeking real-time log redundancy.
🌐 Multiple Tiers — Collector + Analyzer HA
Collector layer per region for log ingestion and archiving. Analyzer layer in HA for analytics. Logs buffer at collector if WAN link fails. Collector mode works on even the lowest FAZ model.
Best for: Large international companies with regional sites needing centralized analytics.
🏗 Multiple Tiers — FortiAnalyzer Fabric
Fabric Supervisor provides centralized visibility across all member FAZ units. Regional SOC teams retain full analyzer capability. Note: No HA on Supervisor in FAZ 7.6 and earlier.
Best for: Multinational customers with regional SOC teams needing local analytics and central oversight.
📊 FortiAnalyzer BigData
Hadoop/Spark/Kafka architecture. Horizontal scalability and built-in data resiliency. Starting at 100,000 LPS. Ideal for high log ingestion, HA, and resiliency at scale.
Best for: Large-scale data centers and high-bandwidth deployments.
